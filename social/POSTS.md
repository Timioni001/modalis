## Posting notes

- **Pin the launch thread.** The video autoplays muted in the feed, so it's built to work without
  sound. Add music in CapCut if you want it for other platforms.
- **Post the images 16:9 as single images.** They're 1600×900, which X shows uncropped.
- **Reply to your own launch post with the pitch video** a day later. It gives the thread a second
  wave without reposting.
- **Keep the claims as they are.** Modalis is on testnet, and the posts say so where it matters.
  "Now on Monad testnet" is on the video's end card for that reason.
