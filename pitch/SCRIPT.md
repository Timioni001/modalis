## Make the "people I love" line yours

That sentence is the heart of the pitch, and judges can hear when a story is borrowed. If it
isn't true for you, swap in the real reason — keep it to one sentence, about a person, not about
the market. The on-screen quote at 0:50 says the same thing, so if you change the words a lot,
tell me and I'll re-render, or cover it in CapCut with a text box.

---

## Recording it

- **Record in one take per section**, not one take for the whole thing. It's far easier to line
  up eleven short clips than to fix one long one.
- A wardrobe or a car is a better booth than an open room: soft things absorb echo.
- Phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly off to the side so your breath doesn't pop.
- Smile on "A clear plan for every goal." People can hear it.

## In CapCut

1. Import `modalis-pitch.mp4` and drop it on the timeline.
2. Import your voice clips and line each one up with the timestamp above.
3. **Audio → Enhance voice** (or *Noise reduction*), then normalise loudness.
4. Add music underneath at **−20 to −24 dB** — quiet enough that it's felt, not heard. Something
   minimal and ambient with a slow pulse; CapCut's library has "cinematic minimal" and
   "technology ambient" packs that fit. Fade it out over the last four seconds.
5. Optional: **Text → Auto captions** — many judges watch muted. Set the style to white, small,
   bottom-centre, so it doesn't fight the on-screen type.
6. Export **1080p, 30fps**.

If a sentence runs long, trim the pause after it rather than speeding your voice up — a rushed
voice reads as nervous. If you're consistently over, the safest line to cut is the
"You say what you're saving for, and when" at 1:05.
